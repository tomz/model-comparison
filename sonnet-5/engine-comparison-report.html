Single-node. DuckDB 1.5.5 is the fastest single-node engine on TPC-H at every scale factor tested. On TPC-DS, Spark Rust 0.42.1 (tuned) is marginally faster than DuckDB in aggregate (0.96× of DuckDB's time), while the default build is roughly at parity. Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2 are both substantially slower than DuckDB and Spark Rust on both suites, with the gap narrowing sharply as scale factor increases (from >40× at SF 1 to roughly 1.7–3.1× at the “all” rollup).
Cluster. At SF 100 and SF 1000, Spark Rust is faster than Spark 4.1.1 Gluten in aggregate wall time and wins the clear majority of individual queries at SF 100 (89/99). At SF 1000 the aggregate gap narrows to roughly parity and the per-query win split is nearly even (48/51 in Gluten's favor by count, even though Rust remains faster in total wall time). At SF 10000 — the largest scale tested — Spark 4.1.1 Gluten becomes faster in aggregate wall time by about 1.47×, while Spark Rust still uses less total CPU-time and memory-time; five of the ninety-nine SF 10000 queries are flagged as unresolved, single-observation, or timed-out on one side of the comparison, so the SF 10000 result should be read with that caveat (see §7).

4. Rollup reconciliation notes

The dataset carries two ratio figures for each “all” rollup row: a ratio recomputed directly from the total-time column, and a matched value cross-checked against the published source markdown. For TPC-H the two agree closely. For TPC-DS they diverge meaningfully for three of the five engines — in particular the default and tuned Spark Rust builds show ratio values at or below 1.0× (implying parity with or a slight edge over DuckDB) while the reconciled matched values are noticeably above 1.0×. Treat the TPC-DS “all” comparison as provisional pending reconciliation with the source report rather than as a settled result.

8. Methodology & provenance

Figures in this report are derived entirely from engine-comparison-summary-data.json (source markdown: engine-comparison-20260925-clean.md, SHA-256 1fe7d64e43e948f991b4636e46579f873411c84d5d5ebbd34a861749ee652d15). Single-node totals sum per-query wall-clock times across the 22 TPC-H or 99 TPC-DS queries at each scale factor; the “all” column aggregates every scale factor tested for that engine. Cluster figures aggregate per-query wall time, CPU-time (core-hours), and memory-time (GB-hours) for the two engines run in the distributed configuration. Ratios are computed as this engine's time ÷ DuckDB's time (single-node) or Gluten's value ÷ Rust's value (cluster); values below 1× indicate the row is faster than, or uses less of that resource than, the reference.

Hardware specifications are self-reported (see §2) and were not independently verified by this report. No values in this document were estimated, extrapolated, or invented — every number traces back to a field in the source JSON.
