Method and notes

Field definitions

Time
Sum of per-query wall-clock seconds for the suite (22 TPC-H or 99 TPC-DS queries).
Ratio
Engine total ÷ baseline total at the same scale factor. Single-node baseline is DuckDB 1.5.5; cluster baseline is Spark 4.1.1 Gluten.
Canonical
Total using only unflagged runs. Where the source reports no canonical total (the total includes flagged runs), it is shown as “not canonical”.
Matched
All-scale-factor ratio restricted to queries with canonical results on both sides.
pass / gap
Cluster status: pass — Spark Rust was at least as fast as Gluten; gap — Gluten was faster. Q39 passes under a ULP (floating-point tolerance) policy.
