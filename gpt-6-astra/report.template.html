04 / Interpretation & provenance

The context behind the numbers.

This report is a presentation of the supplied summary data—not a new benchmark run.

Keep comparisons like-for-like.

Compare engines within the same suite, scale factor and execution mode. Single-node and cluster results use different hardware. “All four scales” sums query times and is dominated by the largest scale; it is not an average speedup or a geometric mean.

Displayed timing values are interpreted as seconds. The JSON has no formal unit dictionary; CPU-hours are identified by the cpuHours field. Other resource values retain their source names where units are unspecified.

Reported is not always canonical.

The source provides time, canonical, and sometimes matched. Their derivation is not documented in this JSON. At TPC-DS SF 1,000, Spark Rust tuned has a reported total of 10,073.977127 and a canonical total of 10,082.325802.

Canonical totals are missing for Spark 4.2 at TPC-H SF 1,000 and Gluten at TPC-DS SF 1,000, as well as their corresponding all-scale totals. Missing values are shown as “—”, never as zero.
