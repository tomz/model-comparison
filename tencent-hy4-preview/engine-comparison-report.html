Executive summary

What the measurements support, stated at the level the data can carry.

Single node: two performance tiers

The five engines split cleanly in two. DuckDB 1.5.5 and Spark Rust 0.42.1 (default and tuned) converge as data grows — within 3% of each other at SF 100 and 5% at SF 1000 on TPC-H — though DuckDB is 33% faster at SF 1. Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2 carry a large fixed per-query cost — every query costs at least 8.8 s at SF 1 regardless of the query — and are 50× to 53× slower on TPC-H at SF 1.

At SF 1 the slowest TPC-H query for Spark 4.2 takes 12.664 s; DuckDB's slowest takes 0.394 s.

The gap closes as data grows

On TPC-H the slowest engine is 53× slower than DuckDB at SF 1 but only 3.11× slower at SF 1000, because their cost is dominated by fixed per-query overhead rather than data volume. The convergence is not monotonic, though: the SF 1000 gap (3.11×) is wider than the SF 100 gap (2.27×).

TPC-DS narrows further: at SF 1000 the fastest and slowest engines are within 1.88× of each other, against 45× at SF 1.

TPC-DS is a genuine three-way contest

On TPC-DS the ranking is not stable. Spark Rust (tuned) is the only configuration to beat DuckDB on summed time (0.963×), Spark Rust (default) is effectively tied (0.994×), and DuckDB wins individual scale factors SF 1 and SF 10 while losing SF 100 and SF 1000. Treat TPC-DS as a tie between the Rust engine and DuckDB, not a DuckDB win.

Cluster: Spark Rust leads until it doesn't

At SF 100 Spark Rust 0.42.1 wins 89 of 99 queries and the wall clock by 1.48×, using 2.7× less CPU. The lead evaporates with scale: 48–51 at SF 1000, and at SF 10 000 Gluten is 1.47× faster overall while Spark Rust still wins 36 queries.

Bottom line. For single-node analytics up to SF 1000, DuckDB 1.5.5 and Spark Rust 0.42.1 are interchangeable on TPC-H and effectively tied on TPC-DS; both are far ahead of Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2, whose advantage is ecosystem compatibility rather than raw speed. For distributed runs, Spark Rust 0.42.1 is the better choice up to about SF 1000, and Spark 4.1.1 Gluten takes over at SF 10 000.
