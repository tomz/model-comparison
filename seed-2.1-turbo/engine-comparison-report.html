Key Findings
  • Small scales favor DuckDB. At SF=1 TPC-H, DuckDB finishes in 4.588 s — Spark Rust default is 1.33× slower, and Spark 4.2 is 50× slower (JVM overhead dominates).
  • Convergence at scale. At SF=1000 TPC-H, DuckDB and Spark Rust are nearly tied (89 vs 92 min, 1.028× ratio).
  • Spark Rust leads TPC-DS at large scale. At SF=1000 TPC-DS, Spark Rust tuned is fastest at 2.80 h, beating DuckDB by 4.2%.
  • Cluster: Spark Rust vs Gluten. At SF=100 on 8 workers, Spark Rust runs in 13.46 min vs Gluten 19.95 min — a 1.48× advantage.
  • Query-level variance is high. Some queries show 10–50× differences between engines, while others are within 10%. See per-query heatmaps below.
