Key Findings

  • Single-node: DuckDB 1.5.5 fastest at SF 1–100. Spark Rust variants within ~3–5% at SF 100–1000. Classic Spark (Gluten/4.2) 2–50× slower at small scale.
  • Cluster (Rust vs Gluten): SF 100: Rust wins 89/99 queries. SF 10k: Gluten wins more queries (58 vs 36) and lower aggregate wall time, but Rust often better p50.
  • Scale behavior: Rust cluster advantage narrows at largest scale; Gluten pulls ahead on heavy queries at SF 10 000.
