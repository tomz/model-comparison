04

Findings & caveats

DuckDB anchors single-node

DuckDB is the ratio = 1.00 baseline on single-node. Spark Rust lands close to it (matched ratios near 1×), while Gluten and vanilla Spark 4.2 trail by multiples at small scale factors.

Scale inverts the cluster ranking

Query-level win counts between Rust and Gluten shift with scale factor — check the wins card for the selected SF before reading the per-query chart as a uniform lead.

Marker letters

Individual repeats carry marker letters (B, P) copied verbatim from the source data. Their meanings are not defined in the dataset, so they are displayed rather than interpreted — a faster wall time carrying markers should be read with caution.

Gaps and baselines

Some rows report gap (result mismatch) or no matched DuckDB baseline. Matched ratios exclude unmatched queries, so totals and matched figures are deliberately different numbers.

Methodology. Single-node times are means of 3 repeats (Spark Rust) and 2 repeats (Gluten) per query; suite totals come from the summary's totals block rather than summing per-query means (they include run overhead). Cluster metrics are as reported by cluster[] / clusterQueries[]. This page is a static artifact — all data is embedded; no scripts or fonts are loaded from the network.
