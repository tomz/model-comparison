    Hardware, definitions & caveats

    Field definitions

    ratio
    Engine time ÷ baseline time — DuckDB on a single node, Gluten on the cluster. Lower is better; 1.00× is parity.
    canonical
    The validated suite total in the source data. Withheld (null) when a query in the run failed validation.
    matched
    Ratio recomputed over the subset of queries with comparable validated results on both engines (reported for the “all” rows).
    diff
    Absolute time difference against the baseline for that comparison.
    B / P markers
    Per-query flags carried in the source data: B blocked or failed validation, P patched-build observation.
    cluster status
    Per-query validation verdict on the cluster: pass (Rust faster, validated), gap (Gluten faster), or one of several “Rust-only” / timeout states where a paired comparison was not possible.
    wins
    Count of resolved paired comparisons won by each engine; queries without a valid paired result are counted as unresolved.
