Key Findings & Insights

1. DuckDB Dominates Across All Workloads

DuckDB 1.5.5 is the consistent performance leader on both TPC-H and TPC-DS benchmarks across all scale factors. It serves as the baseline (1.0×) for all comparisons.

2. Spark Rust Shows Promise as a Spark Alternative

Spark Rust 0.42.1 (both default and tuned configurations) performs remarkably well:

  • TPC-H: Only 2.8% slower than DuckDB on aggregate (6,052 vs 5,883 seconds)
  • TPC-DS: Actually faster than DuckDB on aggregate (10,946 vs 11,369 seconds with tuning)
  • Tuning provides 1–2% additional improvement on TPC-H, 3.3% on TPC-DS
  • Performance gap narrows at larger scale factors (SF 100–1000), suggesting better scalability

3. Traditional Spark Implementations Significantly Underperform

Both Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2 show severe performance degradation:

  • TPC-H: 2.2–3.1× slower than DuckDB (13,153–18,284 seconds)
  • TPC-DS: 1.7–1.8× slower than DuckDB (18,996–19,770 seconds)
  • Performance gap is most severe at small scale factors (SF 1), where Spark is 42–53× slower on TPC-H
  • Gap narrows at larger scales but remains substantial

4. Scale Factor Impact Varies by Engine

  • DuckDB & Spark Rust: Consistent performance ratios across scale factors (1.0–1.05×)
  • Traditional Spark: Dramatic slowdown at SF 1 (50×+ slower), improving at larger scales
  • Suggests Spark has high initialization/overhead costs that amortize at scale

5. TPC-DS Reveals Different Strengths

  • Spark Rust tuned configuration outperforms DuckDB on TPC-DS aggregate (10,946 vs 11,369 seconds)
  • This suggests Spark Rust may have optimizations better suited to complex decision-support queries
  • DuckDB maintains advantage on TPC-H (analytical queries)

6. Tuning Configuration Matters

  • Spark Rust tuned vs. default: 1–3% improvement on TPC-H, 3.3% on TPC-DS
  • Tuning is most effective on TPC-DS at larger scales

Recommendations

For Analytical Workloads (TPC-H)

Primary choice: DuckDB 1.5.5 — Fastest, simplest deployment, no distributed overhead.

Alternative: Spark Rust 0.42.1 (tuned) — If Spark ecosystem integration is required; only 2.4% slower than DuckDB.

For Decision Support Workloads (TPC-DS)

Primary choice: Spark Rust 0.42.1 (tuned) — Outperforms DuckDB by 3.7% on aggregate; better suited to complex queries.

Alternative: DuckDB 1.5.5 — Simpler deployment if marginal performance difference is acceptable.

For Spark Ecosystem Users

Migrate to Spark Rust 0.42.1 — Provides 2.2–3.1× performance improvement over Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2.

Avoid Spark 4.1.1 Gluten and Spark 4.2 — Significant performance penalties across all workloads; no clear advantage over alternatives.

For Scale-Out Scenarios

Both DuckDB and Spark Rust maintain consistent performance ratios across scale factors (1–1000 GB), suggesting predictable scaling behavior. Traditional Spark shows high overhead at small scales but better amortization at larger scales.
